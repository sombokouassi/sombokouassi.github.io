Research

Working Paper · Environmental & Industrial Organization

Environmental Corporate Social Responsibility under Stackelberg Price Competition with R&D

This study examines environmental corporate social responsibility and environmental R&D in a differentiated duopoly with Stackelberg price competition. It investigates how market leadership, R&D cooperation, technological spillovers, and environmental incentives affect firms' strategic behavior and welfare.

Doo-Ri Kim · Sombo E. C. Kouassi · Sang-Ho Lee
Working Paper · Environmental Economics

Technological Asymmetry and Endogenous R&D Cooperation under Emission Taxes vs. Abatement Subsidies

A game-theoretic analysis of environmental policy design when firms differ in abatement technologies and endogenously choose between cooperative and non-cooperative environmental R&D. The study compares emission taxation and abatement subsidies in terms of firms' incentives, emissions, and social welfare.

Doo-Ri Kim · Sombo E. C. Kouassi · Sang-Ho Lee
Research Paper · Applied Econometrics

Labor Market Institutions and CO₂ Emissions

An international panel-data study examining whether the environmental effect of organized labor depends on the renewable-energy context. The analysis combines fixed-effects models, heterogeneous treatment effects, and modern econometric robustness methods.
